EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENT GUIDELINES

1. SUBMISSION PROCESS
All expenses must be submitted through the Expensify portal within 30 days of the transaction. Receipts are required for any expense over $25.

2. TRAVEL
- Flights: Must be booked at least 14 days in advance in Economy class. Business class is only permitted for international flights exceeding 8 hours.
- Hotels: The maximum allowable hotel rate is $250 per night ($350 in Tier 1 cities like New York and San Francisco).
- Meals: Employees receive a per diem of $75 per day for meals while traveling.

3. CLIENT ENTERTAINMENT
Taking clients out for meals or events is permitted. The maximum spend is $150 per person. Alcohol is reimbursable during client dinners, provided it is consumed responsibly.

4. APPROVAL
Expenses under $500 are automatically approved. Expenses over $500 require manager approval. Reimbursements are processed on the 15th and 30th of each month.
