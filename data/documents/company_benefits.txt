EMPLOYEE BENEFITS SUMMARY 2026

HEALTH INSURANCE
The company offers comprehensive health, dental, and vision insurance through BlueCross. The company covers 90% of the premium for the employee and 75% for dependents.

RETIREMENT (401k)
We offer a 401(k) retirement plan through Fidelity. The company matches 100% of employee contributions up to 5% of their base salary. The match vests immediately.

PAID TIME OFF (PTO)
We operate on a "Flexible Time Off" (FTO) policy. While hours are not strictly tracked, employees are encouraged to take a minimum of 4 weeks off per year. All time off must be approved by a direct manager at least 2 weeks in advance.

WELLNESS
Employees receive a monthly wellness stipend of $100 which can be used for gym memberships, meditation apps, or fitness equipment.
