COMPANY REMOTE WORK POLICY
Effective Date: January 1, 2026

1. ELIGIBILITY
All full-time employees who have completed their 90-day probationary period are eligible to request remote work arrangements.

2. ALLOWABLE DAYS
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week. Tuesdays and Thursdays are designated as "Core Collaboration Days" and all employees are expected to be in the office on these days.

3. EQUIPMENT
The company will provide a standard tech stipend of $500 for home office equipment (monitor, keyboard, ergonomic chair). Employees must use company-issued laptops for all work.

4. SECURITY
While working remotely, employees must connect to the corporate VPN at all times. Public Wi-Fi networks may only be used if connected through the VPN.

5. COMMUNICATION
Remote employees must be available on Slack and Zoom during their designated core working hours (10:00 AM to 3:00 PM in their local timezone).
